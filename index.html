A simpler approach

Less chasing. Better shortlists.

Hiring managers should spend time on decisions, not on checking CVs and asking for updates.

Many agencies

CVs sent in bulk

Profiles are matched on keywords and forwarded for you to sort through, so your team ends up doing the first screening.

  • Many profiles that do not fit the role
  • Little checking against the real requirement
  • Updates only when you ask for them
The Tychiff way

A short, checked shortlist

We write the JD with you, check every candidate against it, speak to them first, and send only profiles a recruiter has reviewed.

  • AI score with the evidence, reviewed by a recruiter
  • Interest, notice period and availability confirmed first
  • Live interview and submission status, no chasing
The Tychiff process

Seventeen steps, every time.

Nothing moves forward until the step before it is genuinely done. Steps 1–5 understand the brief, 6–9 search, and 10–17 place and follow up.

  1. RFP requirement shared by client through form
  2. Terms & Conditions
  3. MOU
  4. JD preparation
  5. JD sharing with client & approval
  6. Candidate search: Local DB / Portal DB
  7. Candidate capability validation vs JD (AI tool)
  8. AI score
  9. Discuss candidate interest & availability
  10. Arrange interview
  11. Store resume in Local DB / Cloud
  12. Show recruiter & candidate interview status to client
  13. Recruiter KPI output
  14. Candidate feedback: 2nd day / 1st month
  15. Client feedback: 1st day / 1st month / 2nd month
  16. Invoice raise & follow-up
  17. Requirement closure & thank-you mail
